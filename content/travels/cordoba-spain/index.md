Roman roads, Islamic arches, Christian altars. Córdoba holds them all within itself.
Architecture, flamenco, rich cuisine, and centuries of memories fill the corners.
<!--more-->

---

## The Bridge That Remembers

{{< figure src="images/roman-bridge.jpg" caption="Roman bridge stretching across the Guadalquivir at dusk." >}}

It bears the weight of centuries upon itself as the Guadalquivir slips softly beneath its arches. Once, it was
the only point of connection of the city. So many lives crossed here. Let's imagine the soldiers, the
merchants, the lovers... all of the passed the same worn stones, and the bridge remembers them all.

---

## The Mosque-Cathedral

{{< figure src="images/the-mosque.jpg" caption="Mosque, where the prayers raise" >}}

Beneath these arches, many Gods have been named and praised. Prayers have risen
from different voices, different languages and different rituals, all seeking the same
salvation.

{{< figure src="images/mosque-from-above.jpg" caption="A labyrinth of faith, seen from the above" >}}

---

## The Streets

{{< figure src="images/empty-street.jpg" caption="A street left behind by the crowd" >}}

Streets burn quietly under the sun. Sometimes the air is stifling, as if the stones have retained the
heat of ancient days, and sometimes, in the midst of modernity, we stumble upon a fragment of the past.

{{< figure src="images/roman-temple.jpg" caption="The marble of a Roman temple emerging through modernity" >}}

---

## Where Stone Turned a Hill Into a Fortress

{{< figure src="images/castle-from-distance.jpg" caption="The lone castle" >}}

Seen from afar, the castle looks like a lone sentinel, the Castle of Almodóvar del Río rises against the sky
like a small Atlas, holding up the horizon with silent strength. The white houses at its feet sparkle against
the dark, like pearls on a dark mantle.

{{< figure src="images/castle-from-above.jpg" caption="From the castle’s heights the horizon never ends" >}}
